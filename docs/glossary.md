# Glossary

**Answer class**  
The level requested or served: exact, narrative, or discovery. An automatic request may explicitly downgrade; an exact request may not.

**Authority**  
The review disposition of evidence. Authority does not grant access.

**Citation closure**  
The condition where every material claim maps to current authorized evidence that supports it.

**Document version**  
Immutable content identity independent of a path or filename.

**Evidence span**  
An addressable source passage, page region, table cell, or structured observation.

**Limitation**  
A typed explanation of what the evidence or operation could not support.

**Projection**  
A reproducible view such as an index, summary, wiki page, table, chart, or answer. It is not source authority.

**Retrieval unit**  
A bounded source-backed item used to find evidence.

**Source observation**  
A record of where and when source bytes were observed.

**Structured fact**  
A verified observation with complete entity, metric, period, unit, scale, currency, scope, and citation dimensions.
