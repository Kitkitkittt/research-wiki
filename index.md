<section class="section split">
  <div>
    <div class="eyebrow">THE RESEARCH LOOP</div>
    <h2>Ask broadly.<br>Verify narrowly.</h2>
  </div>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li><strong>Resolve scope.</strong><span>Identify the entity, period, document family, access lane, and requested answer class.</span></li>
    <li><strong>Retrieve safely.</strong><span>Apply permission and current-version filters before ranking any candidate evidence.</span></li>
    <li><strong>Open the source.</strong><span>Fetch the exact cited passage, table cell, page, or structured observation.</span></li>
    <li><strong>Verify the claim.</strong><span>Reject unsupported, stale, partial, or mismatched evidence before synthesis.</span></li>
    <li><strong>State the limit.</strong><span>When the source cannot support the request, return a typed limitation instead of guessing.</span></li>
  </ol>
</section>
