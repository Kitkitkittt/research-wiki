# Citation audit

## Workflow

1. **Inventory** — list every material claim, displayed number, and attached citation.
2. **Re-fetch** — resolve each citation through the current authenticated evidence interface. Cached text is not verification.
3. **Match** — compare the claim with the exact passage, page, row, column, cell, or structured dimensions.
4. **Check version** — detect superseded, historical, missing, unauthorized, or release-changed evidence.
5. **Check numbers** — preserve source sign, digits, unit, scale, currency, period, scope, and actuality.
6. **Classify** — mark each claim `supported`, `partially_supported`, `contradicted`, `unresolved`, or `unauthorized`.
7. **Report** — retain supported text; identify the precise gap for every other claim without inventing a replacement.

## Completion criterion

Complete only when every cited claim has one audit disposition and every `supported` claim points to evidence that directly supports it. Uncited material claims remain unresolved.
