# Company research

## Workflow

1. **Resolve** — map the requested name or symbol to one stable entity identity. Stop on ambiguity.
2. **Scope** — freeze periods, document families, source authority, answer class, and access lane.
3. **Browse** — inspect the entity's document and data inventory before searching so absent coverage stays visible.
4. **Retrieve** — run permission-first evidence search within the frozen scope.
5. **Open** — fetch complete cited passages, pages, tables, and structured observations.
6. **Compare** — preserve revisions, translations, source disagreements, and period differences.
7. **Verify** — audit every material claim and number against current evidence.
8. **Conclude** — separate observations, interpretations, assumptions, risks, catalysts, and limitations.

## Completion criterion

Complete only when the entity is unambiguous, the searched inventory and gaps are stated, and every conclusion has current authorized evidence or an explicit limitation.
