# Period comparison

## Workflow

1. **Resolve periods** — identify fiscal period, reporting frequency, comparison basis, and source version for every side.
2. **Match dimensions** — require compatible entity, metric, scope, assurance, actuality, currency, unit, and scale.
3. **Detect restatements** — prefer explicit comparable-period values where available and preserve both original and restated evidence.
4. **Fetch sources** — open every cited cell or passage from each period.
5. **Compute** — calculate deltas or rates only from verified compatible values; retain precision and formula identity.
6. **Explain** — separate observed change from source-stated drivers and analyst interpretation.
7. **Limit** — mark non-comparable, partial, missing, or conflicting periods instead of forcing a numeric comparison.

## Completion criterion

Complete only when every comparison pair passes the dimension checks and every delta can be reproduced from cited source values. Non-comparable pairs remain explicit.
